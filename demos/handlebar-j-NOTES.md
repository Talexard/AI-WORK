## Facts used (from press and listings; their site blocks automated access, so nothing was taken from it)
- 7116 E Becker Ln, Scottsdale · (480) 948-0110 (from listings). **Confirm the ZIP code, 85254.**
- Herndon family since 1975. Their sons are the Herndon Brothers Band, the house band. (Afar, Herndon family history)
- "Scottsdale's Original Rib House": BBQ ribs and steaks (from a listing).
- Waylon Jennings played there early on; Lyle Lovett, Kris Kristofferson and Vince Gill "drank and sang here" (Phoenix New Times).
- Phoenix New Times Best Country Nightclub, 2011 and 2012.
- Free country dance lessons. **The night varies by source** (Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday). Their own site's pop-up showed "Dance lessons Tuesdays 7pm."
- Reserve ahead for indoor seating on live music nights (from a listing).
