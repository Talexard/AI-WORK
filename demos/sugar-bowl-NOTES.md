**The business:** Sugar Bowl Ice Cream Parlor & Restaurant, 4005 N Scottsdale Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85251 · (480) 946-0051 · sugarbowlscottsdale@yahoo.com

## Everything on the demo comes from their own site
- **History:** opened in 1958 by Jack Huntress; his nephew Carroll took over in the 1980s; today Carroll's daughter and son-in-law run it.
- **Interior:** pink booths, heart-shaped tables, original soda fountain.
- **Fiesta arcade party room** next door: Skee-Ball and a prize Treasure Chest.
- **Family Circus:** Bil Keane featured the Sugar Bowl in his comics. Mentioned in text only. I **did not** use the cartoon images, because they're copyrighted by the cartoon's owners and the Sugar Bowl's right to use them online is unknown.
- **Hours:** parlor and kitchen hours, taken from their contact page.
- **Menu and prices:** from their **January 2025** menu PDFs. **Confirm current prices with the owner.**
- **Photos:** their own photos from their current site, resized for speed. They're small and dated (2018), which is why the design shows them as taped scrapbook snapshots.
- **Press:** "#33 among America's most-saved ice cream shops on Yelp," from a Phoenix New Times article. Re-check the wording before launch.

## Facts to confirm
- **Opening date.** Their "Our Story" page says "New Year's Eve 1958." Their original 1958 ad says "the day before Christmas in '58." The demo says "December 1958." Ask which is right.
- **"68 years" badge.** Dec 1958 to Dec 2026 is 68 years. Their Jan 2025 menu says "celebrating our 68th year," so the count is roughly right either way.

## Placeholders
- 2 review cards. Fill them with real Google reviews, quoted word for word.

## What the new site fixes (pitch points)
1. **The menu is readable on a phone.** Today it's four PDF files. The new menu has tabs and tappable sections.
2. **"Open now" badge** using Arizona time, and today's hours highlighted.
3. **One-tap Call, Menu and Directions** bar on phones.
4. **Fast:** about 0.8 MB with photos, compared with a slow site today (about 11 s to load in our check).
5. **Google-friendly:** business details coded for Google (hours, address, phone, type of business).
6. **A design with as much personality as the parlor:** pink, sprinkles, a chocolate drip, and the family story told properly.
