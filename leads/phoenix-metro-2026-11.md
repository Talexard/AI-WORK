# Leads: Phoenix Metro (East Valley) Businesses That Need a New Website
*Checked 2026-11-06*

**How I found these:**
- Pulled 1,337 members from the Chandler, Gilbert, Scottsdale and Greater Phoenix chamber of commerce directories, in these categories: home services and construction, auto, health and personal care, and restaurants.
- Removed national chains, hospitals and franchise location pages, which left 739 local businesses.
- Loaded every website on a simulated iPhone and checked it for: expired or disconnected domains, sideways scrolling, old layout technology, an outdated © year, missing tap-to-call, and speed.
- Looked at screenshots of the worst ones.

Screenshots: `screenshots/arizona-batch1.png`.
