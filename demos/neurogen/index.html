How HPN works

You relax. Your brain does the work.

High performance neurofeedback (HPN) reads your brainwaves through an EEG and works to interrupt unhealthy patterns, encouraging your brain to form new, healthier ones. It aims at the root of the problem, not just the symptom.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We talk through what you're experiencing and your goals.

  2. 02

    Brain map

    An EEG shows how your brainwaves are behaving. (Confirm the process with the owner)

  3. 03

    Relaxing sessions

    Sit back in a comfortable chair. No pills, no needles, nothing to "do."

  4. 04

    Track progress

    We check in along the way so you can see and feel the changes.
