07 · Questions

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Repairs and small carpentry jobs are welcome. Call and describe the job, and I'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

How do estimates work?

I visit, look at the space and talk through what you want. Then you get a written estimate before any work starts.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job. Call for the current schedule and you'll get a realistic date, not a guess.

Will I be dealing with a big crew?

No. Custom Craft is a solo operation, so you work with the same person from estimate to walkthrough.
