# Peixoto Coffee: Demo Notes
**Files:** `demos/peixoto/` · **Zip:** `demos/peixoto-preview.zip` · **Previews:** `demos/previews/peixoto-*.png`

## Facts used (from peixotocoffee.com)
- **Tagline:** "We Grow. We Roast. We Brew." · "Coffee from our Land to your Life!"
- **Farm:** Fazenda São José da Boa Vista, Minas Gerais, Brazil, at 4,200 ft.
- **History:** Nelson Alvares Peixoto (born 1920) built the farm "Boa Vista." José Augusto (born 1946) planted his own trees at age five and rode a mule to sell at market.
- **Cafés (hours and phones from their Locations page):**
  - Downtown Chandler (flagship): 11 W Boston St #6, (480) 664-6290, open 6–6 daily
  - Epicenter at Agritopia, Gilbert: 3150 E Ray Rd #100, (480) 597-7584, Sun–Thu 6–6, Fri–Sat 6–7
  - Downtown Phoenix, inside ASU 850 PBC: 850 N 5th St #120, (480) 597-6038, 6–6 daily, free parking
- **Coffees and prices:** Familia $23, Doce $23, Honey $24 (out of stock until the 2026 harvest), Boa Vista Dark $22.
- **Subscriptions** save up to 20%. The Roaster's Choice Sampler is $24. Free shipping. Bags are nitrogen-flushed.
- **Wholesale and barista training.**
- **Photos:** all theirs, from their website.

## Confirm before launch
- **"4.7★ from 1,400+ reviews"** comes from Birdeye (about 1,448 reviews). Use their Google number if it's better.
- **The tasting notes** come from their bag labels and product pages.
- **Shop buttons** link to their **existing WooCommerce store**, so we keep their checkout. Pitch this as a new front end and brand experience, not a store rebuild.

## Pitch angle
Their current site works but looks dated: it shows "© 2016" and has a stock Instagram feed. **Their story is the best in Arizona coffee** (they grow their own beans), and the current site hides it.

The demo tells the journey from farm to cup, shows every café with a live "open now" status, and gives subscriptions their own section, since subscriptions are recurring revenue for them.
