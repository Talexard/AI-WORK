Our story

Old Town changed. We didn't.

Jack Huntress opened the bubble-gum pink parlor in December 1958 to give Scottsdale families somewhere fun to eat. Pink booths, heart-shaped tables and the original soda fountain are still here.

  1. 1958
    Jack Huntress opens the doors. His opening ad promised goodies "to tickle your tummy."
  2. 1980s
    His nephew Carroll takes over, and keeps every recipe.
  3. Today
    Carroll's daughter and son-in-law run Old Town's most colorful restaurant.
✎

Famous in the funny pages. Paradise Valley cartoonist Bil Keane featured the Sugar Bowl many times in The Family Circus. Look for his drawings on our walls, with prints available in the gift shop.

Next door: the Fiesta

Birthday parties, done sweet.

Sundaes first, then Skee-Ball and arcade games in the Fiesta room next door. Kids trade their winnings at the Treasure Chest, and you don't clean up a thing.
