Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Repairs and small carpentry jobs are welcome. Call and describe the job, and I'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

How do estimates work?

I visit the home, look at the space and talk through what you want. Then you get a written estimate so you know what to expect before any work starts.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job. Call for the current schedule. I'll give you a realistic start date, not a guess.

Will I be dealing with a big crew?

No. Custom Craft is a solo operation, so you work with the same person from the estimate to the final walkthrough.

Ready to get your project started?

Call or text for the fastest answer, or send a request and I'll get back to you.
