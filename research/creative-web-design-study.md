# Study: What Makes Modern Websites Eye-Catching (Nov 2026)

I took screenshots of 15 sites and looked at them closely: 6 tech brands known for design (Linear, Stripe, Raycast, Family, Framer, Apple) and 9 builder/remodeler sites from 2026 "best contractor website" lists (Anotherkind, Bellweather Design Build, Schmitt & Co, Thomas James Homes, Majestic Tiles, Zicklin, Dyna Builders, Eden Builders, West Village GC). I also read a handful of 2026 trend round-ups.
Contact sheets: `inspiration/tech-brands.png`, `inspiration/builders.png`.

## The 8 patterns that make sites memorable

1. **Huge, confident type as the main visual.** Anotherkind sets its name edge to edge in bright orange. Bellweather's name is about 130px tall and mixes upright and *italic* letters. Linear and Raycast use 64px+ headlines. Big type grabs attention without needing photos.
2. **Mixing roman and italic serif letters.** "Bell*weather*", "LUXURY HOME REMODELING *in Chicago*". It reads as editorial, crafted and upscale. A very common 2026 look.
3. **Warm off-white instead of pure white.** Anotherkind uses cream (#FCF9EB), and Bellweather uses warm beige. It feels like paper and craft rather than software.
4. **One loud accent color, used sparingly.** Anotherkind's orange-red, Stripe's gradient, Raycast's red. Everything else stays neutral.
5. **Playful details that show personality.** Anotherkind's spinning circular badge, Family's illustrated characters. These small touches make a site feel designed rather than built from a template.
6. **Motion that responds to the visitor.** Content fades in as you scroll, lines draw themselves, text scrolls past in a ticker. Restrained, not flashy. Every top site uses some form of it.
7. **Photo collages and full-width photos.** Majestic Tiles shows 4–5 project photos in a grid. Schmitt and Dyna use one full-screen photo. *For trades, real project photos are the strongest asset.*
8. **Lots of empty space plus a strict layout grid.** Elements sit on clear columns, with generous spacing between sections.

## What the builder sites get wrong (and what we can beat)
- Cookie banners covering the hero (Majestic, Thomas James).
- Blank screens while slow videos or animations load (West Village GC showed only a loading spinner).
- Fancy sites with no phone number or call button on the first screen (Schmitt, Bellweather).
- Generic templates with stock hero photos and "Industry-Leading" headlines (Eden).

**Our formula:** big editorial type + warm palette + one bold accent + a few playful details + subtle scroll motion, **and** the sales basics on screen at all times (phone number, estimate button, trust details). Keep it fast and light, with no heavy code libraries.

## Applied to small local trades
- When a client has few photos, **type, color and graphic details carry the design.** Use themed graphics that fit the trade, such as tape-measure ticks, blueprint grids and line drawings.
- Write the business's own story in the headline. "Industry-leading" says nothing.
- Respect the phone's "reduce motion" setting, and make sure every piece of content still shows if animations don't run.
