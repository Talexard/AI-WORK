# Pitch Script: Handlebar J Restaurant & Saloon

**Phone:** (480) 948-0110 · **Address:** 7116 E Becker Ln, Scottsdale
**Ask for:** the owner or general manager. The Herndon family has run it since 1975, and their sons are the Herndon Brothers Band.
**Demo link:** `[your Netlify link, e.g. handlebarj-preview.netlify.app]`
**Price:** $1,800 one-time, or $1,500 for a testimonial. Add-ons: photo shoot +$400; weekly lineup updates $50 per update, or teach them to do it themselves.

## Best time
- **Call Tuesday–Thursday, about 2–4 pm**, before the evening crowd. One listing says they're closed Mondays.
- **Best of all, go in person on a weeknight before the band starts.** Order something, enjoy it, then ask for the owner. A bar is the easiest place to pitch face to face.

## Call
**Opener:**
> "Hi, is the owner or manager around? My name is [Your name], I'm a local web designer. I put together something for Handlebar J. Two minutes, tops."

**With the owner:**
> "I've got to say, a place where Waylon Jennings played, that's been family-run since 1975 and named Best Country Nightclub by New Times, is a real Scottsdale legend. But when I pulled up your website on my phone, I got a dark page with a pop-up and couldn't tell who was playing tonight or how to get a table."
>
> "So I built a free sample of a new site. Can I text it to you? It's best on your phone."

**While they look:**
> "The big sign up top is like your real one, with marquee lights and neon. Right below is 'Now playing,' a weekly lineup you can update, with tonight highlighted. Then the free dance lessons, your ribs, and a wall of the legends who've played there. And there's a big 'Call to reserve' button everywhere, because show nights fill up."

**Ask, then listen:**
- "How do people find out who's playing each week? Facebook, word of mouth?"
- "Do you get a lot of calls asking about the schedule or dance lessons?"
- "Do you do private parties or group nights? Would you want more?"

**Offer and close:**
> "It's a one-time price of $1,800, no monthly fees to me. You own it. I'll add your real menu, schedule and photos, and it's live in about a week. Want me to set it up?"

## Voicemail
> "Hi, this is [Your name], a local web designer. I built a free sample website for Handlebar J that shows who's playing each night and makes it easy to reserve a table. I'll text you the link. My number is [your number]. Thanks!"

## Text after a voicemail
> Hi! [Your name] here, a local web designer. I built a free sample site for Handlebar J: [link]. It shows the week's lineup, dance lessons, the ribs and the legends who've played there, with one-tap reserve. No obligation. Hope you like it!

## Objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We post everything on Facebook." | "Perfect, keep doing that. But tourists and new folks Google you first. This makes sure they see tonight's band and a call button, not a pop-up." |
| "We've been packed for 50 years." | "You have, and the site should look as legendary as the place. It also cuts down the 'who's playing?' calls." |
| "Too expensive." | "It's one-time, with no monthly fees, and I can split it into two payments. A couple of extra group reservations covers it." |
| "Talk to my brother / my family." | "Of course. It's a family place. I'll text you the link to share. Can I check back Thursday?" |

## After a yes, collect
- The real weekly schedule and the dance lesson night
- Menu and prices; hours
- Photos (stage, dance floor, ribs, the sign) or permission for a photo shoot
- Who manages the domain (their current site sits behind Cloudflare, so someone has an account)
