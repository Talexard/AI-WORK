From our land to your life

Most roasters buy coffee. We grow ours.

  1. 01 · Grow

    On the family farm

    Coffee trees on the hills of Fazenda Boa Vista, 4,200 feet above the Rio Grande valley.

  2. 02 · Harvest & dry

    Picked and sun-dried

    Microlots hand-picked on slopes too steep for machines, then dried on the patio.

  3. 03 · Roast

    Roasted with intention

    Imported by the family and roasted in small batches to bring out every bit of sweetness.

  4. 04 · Brew

    Poured in Arizona

    Served in our three Valley cafés or shipped to your door, nitrogen-flushed for freshness.

100+ years of coffee

A family trade, passed down.

  1. 1920

    Nelson Alvares Peixoto is born among the coffee trees, and later builds the farm he calls Boa Vista, "good view."

  2. 1950s

    José Augusto, age five, plants his own coffee trees and rides a mule three hours to sell his harvest at market.

  3. Today

    The family grows, imports, roasts and brews its own coffee, from our land to your life.
