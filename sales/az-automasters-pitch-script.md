# Pitch Script: AZ AutoMasters (Chandler & Gilbert)

**Call:** Chandler **480-592-9677** (start here). Gilbert 480-545-1998.
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 7:30 am–6 pm, Sat 8–3. **Best time:** 2–5 pm on weekdays. Avoid early morning drop-offs (7:30–9) and lunch.
**Ask for:** the owner or manager.
**Demo link:** `[your Netlify link, e.g. azautomasters-preview.netlify.app]`
**Price:** $2,000 one-time. Two shops plus a booking form; offer $1,700 if they give a testimonial. Optional photo shoot +$400.

## Call
**Opener:**
> "Hi, is the owner or manager around? My name is [Your name], a local web designer. I put together something for AZ AutoMasters. It'll take two minutes."

**With the owner:**
> "You guys have a 4.8 on Google with over 500 reviews across both shops. That's rare for a repair shop. But I pulled up your website on my phone, and the first thing you see is a wall of menu buttons. Nothing says how good you are or makes it easy to call. A lot of people decide which shop to call in about five seconds on their phone."
>
> "So I built a free sample of a new site. Can I text it to you? It's best on your phone."

**While they look:**
> "Up top it shows your 4.8 rating and a call button for each shop. Then your free check-engine scan, free inspection, towing and the 2-year warranty, right where people see them. There's an appointment form where customers pick Chandler or Gilbert, and it shows 'open now' with your hours."

**Ask:**
- "How do most new customers find you? Google, referrals, drive-bys?"
- "Do people ever call the wrong shop?"
- "Who handles your website now?"

**Offer and close:**
> "It's a one-time price of $2,000, no monthly fees to me. You own it, I add your real reviews and confirm your hours, and it's live in about a week. One extra brake job or AC repair a month pays for it. Want me to set it up?"

## Voicemail
