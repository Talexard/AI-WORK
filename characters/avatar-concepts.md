# AI Avatar Concepts: Round 1

**Brief:** An original AI avatar in the same wave as Jean Phil, Clarabelle, Margot Marmalade and the viral "Lord Farquaad" dancer, but **not a copy**. The character is **Black, with a European hairstyle**, and has to grab attention on its own.

---

## 1. What the reference videos have in common

These are the parts that work and that we keep:

| Element | In the references | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **The hair is the logo** | Blond bob, orange bob, black pageboy | Readable as a thumbnail in half a second. You know who it is before you see the face |
| **An old-fashioned costume in a modern, ordinary place** | Houndstooth suit at an office park; medieval velvet at a bus stop and on a Paris street | The time-travel contrast is the joke. No dialogue needed |
| **Deadpan face, unexpected movement** | Serious face + shadowboxing, dancing, Smooth Criminal | The contrast between the serious face and the silly movement |
| **Silent, carried by music** | Trending audio, no talking | Works in every language, easy to remix |
| **Real footage underneath** | Real streets, real light (motion transfer / swap) | Lighting matches automatically, so it looks real |
| **A shared universe** | "On my way, Jean. #jeanphilanthrope" | Characters reference each other, which builds lore and cross-traffic |
| **Relatable caption** | "us after pretending to work for 8 hours" | Turns a weird clip into something people send to friends (Clarabelle: **6,095 shares**) |

### What we do **differently**

- **Not** a pale blond or orange bob. Our character is **Black**, and the hair is a **bold European historical style**. That combination alone has not been done in this wave, and it's a strong, high-contrast silhouette.
- **Not** only "deadpan dancing." Each concept below has its **own signature action**, so it's a new format, not "another Jean Phil."
- **Don't use** the houndstooth suit + blond bob combo, or Lord Farquaad (that's DreamWorks' character, so it's a copyright risk and not ours).

### Ground rule for all five

The character is **the most elegant, confident person in every scene**. The humor comes from the **era clashing with the modern world**, never from race. There have been Black Europeans in every one of these eras, so this is a character with style and not a costume joke. That also makes it far more shareable and brand-safe.

## 2. The five concepts

### Concept A: **Baptiste de Valmont**, the Baroque duelist

| | |
|---|---|
| **Era / hair** | 1770s Versailles. A **tall powdered-white periwig** with rolled side curls and a black silk ribbon tying the back |
| **Look** | Dark brown skin, pale mint or champagne silk frock coat with gold embroidery, a lace jabot at the neck, white stockings, buckled shoes, a thin fencing sword (smallsword) |
| **Personality** | A proud, extremely polite aristocrat. Offended by modern life |
| **Signature action** | **Challenges modern annoyances to a duel.** Salutes with the sword, lunges "en garde" at a parking meter, a self-checkout or a scooter blocking the sidewalk, then gives a deep bow and walks away victorious |
| **Why it stops the scroll** | White powdered wig on dark skin = the **highest-contrast silhouette of the five**. You'll spot it from across the For You page |
| **Series hooks** | "Baptiste vs. the self-checkout," "Baptiste vs. Monday," "Baptiste vs. the group chat" |
| **Sample captions** | "when the printer says 'paper jam' but there is no paper jam" · "me defending my lunch in the office fridge" |
| **Music** | Baroque harpsichord remixes, dramatic classical drops, trending sounds with a classical twist |

**Production notes**
- **Framing:** the wig adds a lot of height. Leave **20–25% headroom**. Film full body so the lunge and footwork read.
- **Movement:** fencing moves are slow and big, which is **ideal for Genjutsu** (clear motion, hands visible). Use a cheap foam sword or a stick as the prop so the AI has something to swap.
- **Lighting:** a silk coat and white wig show light direction very clearly, so this is the concept where lighting errors are most visible. Film in soft overcast light or golden hour and use **Object Swap** so your real light is kept.

### Concept B: **Sir Reginald Ashworth**, the Victorian racewalker

| | |
|---|---|
| **Era / hair** | 1900s London. **Slick center-part pomade hair** that shines, with a **huge waxed handlebar mustache** curled at the ends |
| **Look** | Deep brown skin, a brown tweed three-piece suit, a pocket watch on a chain, an old-style number bib pinned to the chest ("No. 7"), arm garters, a stopwatch |
| **Personality** | A deadly serious 1908 Olympic racewalker who thinks every walk is a competition |
| **Signature action** | **Power-walks through modern life at race speed.** Hips swinging, elbows pumping, overtaking people on scooters, escalators and airport travelators, checking his pocket watch, never breaking into a run |
| **Why it stops the scroll** | The racewalk itself is instantly funny, and **nobody in this wave is doing it**. A totally new format |
| **Series hooks** | "Reginald vs. the airport," "Reginald racing the bus," "Reginald's commute times" |
| **Sample captions** | "me when the group chat says 'outside in 5'" · "walking to the fridge after hearing the microwave beep" · "me leaving work at 4:59" |
| **Music** | Upbeat ragtime, 1900s silent-film piano, fast trending audio |

**Production notes**
- **Easiest to perform.** Anyone can racewalk. Film it on a sidewalk or in a park. The motion is repetitive and clear, so Genjutsu handles it well.
- **Framing:** a **tracking shot** (walk backwards with the phone on a gimbal) or a **side-on pan**. Keep the feet in frame so the silly walk reads, with space in front of him in the direction he's walking.
- **Lighting:** outdoor daylight, so film at a consistent time of day for the series. The pomade shine should pick up a highlight from the same side as the sun.

### Concept C: **Odette Marceau**, the 1960s Parisian critic

| | |
|---|---|
| **Era / hair** | 1963 Paris, New Wave cinema. A **big glossy black bouffant with a full fringe**, a silk headscarf tied under the chin, and cat-eye eyeliner |
| **Look** | Rich dark skin, a Breton striped top under a cream trench coat, cigarette pants, flat ballet shoes, oversized sunglasses pushed up, and **a small handheld scorecard** |
| **Personality** | Chic, unimpressed, impossible to please. A French film critic judging everything |
| **Signature action** | **Silently rates things.** She looks at something (a sandwich, a fountain, someone's outfit, a TikTok trend), pauses, slowly lowers her sunglasses, then **holds up a score card: "2/10."** Rarely, a "10/10" moment that goes viral by itself |
| **Why it stops the scroll** | **The whole format is about the comments.** People argue with the score, suggest things for her to rate, and wait for the rare 10. That drives comments and shares |
| **Series hooks** | "Odette rates your city," "Odette rates American breakfast," "Odette rates your comments" (reply-to-comment videos) |
| **Sample captions** | "Odette has seen the new coffee shop" · "rate my outfit? Odette already did" |
| **Music** | Yé-yé 1960s French pop, cool jazz, trending sounds |

**Production notes**
- **Framing:** mostly **medium shots** (waist up) so the face, sunglasses and card read. A bouffant needs **~20% headroom**. The card must be held **inside the frame, away from the bottom-right app buttons**.
- **Movement:** small and slow (a look, a pause, a card lift), which is very easy for Genjutsu to keep stable. Fewer hand artifacts than any dance.
- **Make the card real**: write the score on real card in the source video. AI-generated text often warps. Or add the number in the edit.
- **Lighting:** she's often in front of a sunny street or café window, so keep the key light on her face from the same side as the scene. Watch her sunglasses: they should reflect the street, not a studio.

### Concept D: **Colette Duval**, the silent-film star lost in 2026

| | |
|---|---|
| **Era / hair** | 1926 Paris. A **glossy black finger-wave bob**, sculpted flat to the head in S-shaped waves, with a jeweled headband |
| **Look** | Warm dark skin, a beaded fringe flapper dress, long pearls, T-strap heels, dramatic 1920s makeup (dark lips, thin brows) |
| **Personality** | A silent movie star who woke up in 2026 and reacts to everything with **big, over-the-top 1920s silent-film acting** |
| **Signature action** | **The whole video is a silent film.** Black-and-white, sped up a bit, with film flicker and **intertitle cards** ("She had never seen… a vape."). She faints at a Tesla, gasps at a phone, clutches her pearls at a self-driving car |
| **Why it stops the scroll** | The **black-and-white silent-movie look** sticks out in a full-color feed, and the intertitles let you tell a real joke without speaking. **A visual format nobody in this wave uses** |
| **Series hooks** | "Colette discovers…" (the gym, a smart speaker, oat milk, dating apps) |
| **Sample captions** | "Colette has discovered the self-checkout. She is not well." |
| **Music** | Silent-film piano; ragtime covers of trending songs |

**Production notes**
- **Film in color and make it black-and-white in the edit**, so the lighting from Genjutsu matches first. Then add grain, flicker, a slight speed-up and the intertitle cards.
- **Framing:** full body for the big dramatic poses (faints, swoons). The finger-wave bob is **flat**, so it needs only normal headroom. The easiest hair to swap cleanly.
- **Lighting:** 1920s films used **hard, dramatic light**. Film with one strong light from the side (a window or a lamp), and black-and-white will make it look period-correct.
- **Lighting still matters in black-and-white**: the brightness and shadow direction must still match between her and the background.

### Concept E: **Dieter Okonkwo-Braun**, the 1983 Berlin synth-pop star

| | |
|---|---|
| **Era / hair** | 1980s Berlin new wave. A **dramatic asymmetrical platinum wedge**: shaved on one side, a long swooping fringe covering one eye |
| **Look** | Deep dark skin, a black leather jacket with huge shoulder pads over a white mesh top, narrow sunglasses, one fingerless glove, a **keytar** strapped on |
| **Personality** | A cold, serious German synth-pop artist. Treats every everyday moment like a music video |
| **Signature action** | **Scores the moment.** Something ordinary happens (the toast pops, the elevator arrives, the microwave beeps), and he plays a **dramatic synth sting on the keytar** with a slow, robotic head turn to camera |
| **Why it stops the scroll** | A bold, bright hair shape against dark skin. A **sound-first format**: the synth stings become his **audio trademark**, and people can reuse his sound in their own videos, which spreads it |
| **Series hooks** | "Dieter scores your day," reuse the synth sting as an original sound so other people make videos with it |
| **Sample captions** | "the microwave has finished. Dieter has feelings." · "when the uber is 1 minute away for 9 minutes" |
| **Music** | Original synth stings (make your own = your own sound), 80s synth-pop |

**Production notes**
- **Framing:** the platinum wedge is tall on one side, so leave **~20% headroom** and extra room on that side. Medium shots to show the keytar and hands.
- **Hands on the keytar** move a lot: keep the movements slow and keep hands in frame, or film the keytar close-ups separately.
- **Lighting:** a great chance for **colored lighting** (pink, blue, neon), but it only looks real if the colored light is **really there when you film** (cheap RGB light bulbs or LED strips). Colored light added only by the AI is the easiest way to get the "pasted-on" look.

## 3. Review: side by side

Scores are 1–5 (5 = best).

| | A. Baptiste (Baroque duelist) | B. Reginald (racewalker) | C. Odette (60s critic) | D. Colette (silent film) | E. Dieter (synth-pop) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Scroll-stopping silhouette** | **5** (white wig, dark skin) | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **Different from Jean Phil's crew** | 4 | **5** | **5** | **5** | 4 |
| **Easy to film and to swap in Genjutsu** | 3 (tall wig, sword) | **5** | **5** | 4 | 3 (hands on keys) |
| **Easy to match lighting** | 3 (silk and white show every mistake) | 4 | 4 | **5** (B&W forgives) | 2 (colored light) |
| **Comments and shares potential** | 4 | 4 | **5** (people argue with scores) | 4 | 4 (reusable sound) |
| **Can run as a long series** | 4 | 4 | **5** | 4 | 4 |
| **Total (of 30)** | 23 | 25 | **28** | 26 | 21 |

### My recommendation

1. **Odette (C)** is the strongest overall. It's the easiest to produce well, the movement is small and stable, and the rating format **makes people comment**. That's the signal the algorithm rewards, and it can run forever ("Odette rates…" anything, including your commenters' requests).
2. **Colette (D)** is the most original *look*. The black-and-white silent-film style will stand out in any feed, and it hides small lighting mismatches.
3. **Baptiste (A)** has the **most striking single image**. If thumbnail impact is the #1 goal, it wins, but it's the hardest to get looking real.

**A strong play:** start with **Odette** as the main character, then later introduce another one as a **crossover**. Odette rates Baptiste's duel, or Colette meets Odette in Paris. That builds the same kind of shared universe that makes "On my way, Jean" work.

## 4. Next steps once you pick one

1. **Make the reference pack**: 4–6 images of the character (front, 3/4, full body, face close-up), evenly soft-lit on a plain background. The starter prompt is below.
2. **Film 3 test clips** of yourself doing the signature action (10–15 s each, steady camera, bright light, full framing with headroom).
3. **Run them through Genjutsu** (Object Swap to keep your real light), check them with the [playbook checklist](../guides/seedance-2-5-and-genjutsu-playbook.md#part-5-the-final-checklist-every-clip), and pick the best.
4. **Post 3 episodes within the first week**, all in the same format, and see which one gets the most completions and shares.

### Starter prompt for the reference image (example: Odette)

```
Full-body fashion photograph of a Black woman with rich dark skin in her late 20s, a big glossy black 1960s bouffant hairstyle with a full straight fringe, a silk headscarf tied under the chin, sharp cat-eye eyeliner. She wears a Breton striped top, an open cream trench coat, black cigarette pants and flat ballet shoes, oversized sunglasses pushed up on her head, holding a small white score card. Standing straight, facing the camera, neutral unimpressed expression. Plain light-grey studio background, soft even front lighting, no harsh shadows, no filters, sharp focus, natural skin texture, 9:16.
```

Swap in the details from any other concept's "Look" row to make that character's pack. Keep the last line (plain background, soft even light, no filters) the same for every character, so the references relight cleanly in Genjutsu.
