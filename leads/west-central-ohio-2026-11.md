# Leads: Local Businesses Whose Websites Need Work
*West-central Ohio (Urbana / Bellefontaine / Champaign & Logan counties), checked 2026-11-03*

**How these were found:** I took about 45 contractor and trade websites listed in the Champaign County and Logan County chamber of commerce directories, plus a few in the Dayton area. Each site was loaded on a simulated iPhone and checked for phone layout, page weight, outdated technology, click-to-call, contact forms and reviews. I then looked at screenshots of each one. Screenshots: `screenshots/batch1.png`, `screenshots/batch2.png`. Raw data: `audit-results-2026-11-03.json`.
