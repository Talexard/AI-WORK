## Description
- **Face and body:** young (early 20s) dark-skinned Black man, clear smooth glowing skin, long slender face, high cheekbones, sharp narrow jaw, long slim neck, tall and slim
- **Hair:** soft off-black, silky smooth trapezoid bob. Flat smooth crown, soft wispy fringe curving just above the eyebrows, full panels widening to the jaw, ends rolled under with a soft bell swing, satin sheen, no frizz.
- **Mustache:** off-black sculpted handlebar with the ends curled into small loops, same color as the hair
- **Outfit:** slightly boxy 1970s navy-and-camel windowpane-check sport coat, cream pointed-collar shirt, striped regimental tie
- **Expression:** deadpan

## House style
- Raw smartphone look: phone propped low on a table or counter, or a close selfie (phone not in shot)
- Ordinary real places: café terrace (croissant and espresso in the foreground), apartment kitchen, sidewalks
- Soft overcast daylight or plain warm indoor light
- Stiff deadpan dance moves: rolling arms, one-arm swings

## Prompt pitfalls (learned the hard way)
- Never write "TikTok" or "viral video frame". The model draws fake app screens over the image.
- "Gaunt / hollow cheeks / heavy-lidded" makes him look old. Use "young, clear glowing skin".
- "HUGE voluminous" makes a mushroom or dome. Describe a flat crown with fullness only at the bottom.
- "Fluffy" makes the hair crimped. Use "silky smooth".
- "Selfie" puts a phone in the shot. Say "front-camera point of view, phone not visible".
- Avoid a raised-fist gesture. It reads as a political salute.
